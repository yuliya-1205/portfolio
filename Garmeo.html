Garmeo
Garmeo is an online platform where customers can select garments and upload designs for printing or embroidery.

As a graphic designer for the Garmeo brand, I created engaging social media graphics that resonated with the target audience, established consistent branding elements, designed web assets to enhance the digital experience, and produced advertising materials that effectively conveyed the brand's values. My contributions ensured a strong and cohesive visual representation of Garmeo across all platforms
